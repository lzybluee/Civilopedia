World's Fair

Summary:

Begins the World's Fair project. Once underway, Civilizations can contribute Production towards its completion by selecting it in the city production list. When complete, Civilizations receive bonuses based on how much they contributed.

: Culture increases by 100% for 20 Turns.
: Free Social Policy.
: 500 points towards next Golden Age.
